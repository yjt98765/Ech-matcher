## Arithmetic benchmark circuits

The circuit files in `artifact/data/` were obtained from the
[`Arithmetic_and_Toffoli` directory][source] of the `njross/optimizer`
repository. Their original filenames ended in `_after_heavy`; that suffix was
removed in this artifact.

The associated work is:

Y. Nam, N. J. Ross, Y. Su, A. M. Childs, and D. Maslov, "Automated
optimization of large quantum circuits with continuous parameters," *npj
Quantum Information*, vol. 4, article 23, 2018.
